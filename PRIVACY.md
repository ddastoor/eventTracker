# Event Tracker — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 10, 2026_

Event Tracker is a personal Android app for logging events and charting how often they happen.
This policy explains what the app accesses and where your data goes.

## What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for two permissions:

- **See, edit, create, and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app**
  (`drive.file`). The app uses this to create and update its own "Event Tracker" folder,
  spreadsheets, and the attachments you add. It **cannot** see any other files in your Drive.
- **See your primary Google Account email address** (`userinfo.email`). This is only shown in
  the app so you know which account is signed in.

## Where your data is stored

- **In your own Google Drive**: your event types, events, notes, and attached files are saved
  in the "Event Tracker" folder in your Google account.
- **On your phone**: the app keeps a copy so it works quickly and offline.

There is **no Event Tracker server**. Your data goes directly between your phone and your own
Google account. The developer never receives, sees, or stores your data, and the app contains
no analytics, advertising, or tracking. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Deleting your data

- Sign out in the app (Settings → Sign out) to remove the copy on your phone, or uninstall the app.
- Delete the "Event Tracker" folder from your Google Drive to remove the stored data.
- Remove the app's access to your Google account at
  <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.
